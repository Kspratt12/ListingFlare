## 1. NAR statistics checked against the original reports
Original source read: NAR's own pages for the **2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers** (news item dated 2025-11-04 and economist takeaways dated 2025-11-03). Survey period **July 2024 to June 2025**; population **primary-residence buyers** (not vacation or investor purchases); sample size and response rate are not stated on those pages. Verified from them: FSBO **5%** of home sales ("an all-time low" / "historic low of 5% of sellers"); median sale price **$360,000 FSBO vs $425,000 agent-assisted** (these are different homes, so it does not measure what an agent adds); **91%** of sellers used an agent; **88%** of buyers purchased through an agent; agents described as the most trusted information source, ahead of online listings. **Not on those pages:** the share who found their home online, the share who used the internet in the search, and referral shares.

## 3. Packaging: what was verified and changed
- **Flyers:** trial flag aligned to the existing behavior (`canGenerateOpenHouseFlyer: true`); nothing in the UI or server ever gated flyers, so no user sees a change.
- **5-lead cap:** the unused \`maxLeads\` setting was referenced only in \`subscription.ts\` (checked across src); removed. No inquiry is hidden or dropped.
- **AI description (unchanged):** route \`/api/ai/generate\` requires a signed-in user whose subscription is active or whose trial is live; **20 requests per 10 minutes per user** (an in-memory, per-serverless-instance limiter, so best-effort); output capped at **1,024 tokens**; model \`claude-sonnet-5-5\` (overridable with \`ANTHROPIC_MODEL\`, with fallbacks); **input fields are not clipped** (the public chat route clips its inputs; this one does not), so per-call input cost is bounded only by the request size limit. **No per-account or global spend controls exist in the application code**; whether the Anthropic account has a spend limit cannot be seen from here. The "1 AI description" allowance in the trial remains unenforced. Separate proposal (not implemented): clip input fields to sensible lengths (behavior-preserving for normal use) and, if spend data warrants, a usage counter.
- Other AI routes: assistant 30/10 min, caption 30/10 min, extract-listing 20/10 min, listing-presentation 10/10 min, social-caption 15/10 min (all per user, max_tokens 700 to 1,500).
